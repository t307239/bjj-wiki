    Perguntas Frequentes

    Como eu evito que meu oponente me passe com o double under?

    Para evitar o double under, mantenha a base baixa e larga, use seus quadris para criar pressão contra o oponente e tente manter os joelhos afastados. Se ele conseguir fechar os braços, tente criar espaço com os cotovelos ou um quadril.

    Qual a melhor forma de finalizar o double under pass para obter a montada ou as costas?

    Após controlar os braços com o double under, você pode usar a pressão do seu corpo para empurrar os quadris do oponente para o lado, abrindo caminho para a montada. Alternativamente, se ele tentar defender de costas, você pode usar a pegada nos braços para girar e buscar as costas.

    O que fazer se meu oponente defender o double under pass com um 'guard retention' forte?

    Se o oponente usar um bom 'guard retention', como um 'knee shield' ou 'half guard', você precisará ajustar sua pressão e ângulo. Tente usar o peso do seu corpo para quebrar a defesa, ou procure transições para outras posições de passe, como o 'over-under' ou 'torre'.

    Common Mistakes in Double Under Pass

    Rushing the Setup

    Attempting to finish before proper mechanics are in place results in failed attempts and positional loss. Prioritize position before submission.

    Using Strength Over Technique

    Muscling through setups creates bad habits and fails against stronger or more skilled opponents. Focus on leverage and angles.

    Skipping Drilling

    Techniques only become available in live rolling after extensive drilling. Regular repetition builds the muscle memory needed for execution under pressure.

    Ignoring Defensive Reactions

    Every technique has common counters. Learn the most frequent defensive reactions and have follow-up attacks ready.

    Training Tips for Double Under Pass

    Shadow Drill at Full Speed

    Perform the technique slowly, then progressively increase to competition speed while maintaining crisp mechanics. Video yourself to catch form breakdowns.

    Use a Skilled Partner

    Training with a partner who can give realistic resistance and honest feedback accelerates technical development more than repetitions with a passive uke.

    Isolate Weak Phases

    Break the technique into phases and identify which phase breaks down under pressure. Spend disproportionate drilling time on that specific phase.

    Compete in Tournaments

    Competition reveals real weaknesses that controlled training obscures. Even white belts benefit from early competitive experience.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does it take to learn Double Under Pass?

    Most practitioners develop functional competency with Double Under Pass within 3–6 months of consistent drilling. Mastery — the ability to execute reliably in live rolling against resisting opponents — typically takes 1–2 years.

    Is Double Under Pass effective for beginners?

    Yes. Double Under Pass is part of the core BJJ curriculum and taught at all belt levels. Beginners should focus on the fundamental mechanics and concepts before refining advanced entries.

    How often should I drill Double Under Pass?

    3–5 times per week is ideal for rapid skill acquisition. Even 10 focused repetitions per session compounds over time — consistency matters more than volume.

    What positions connect to Double Under Pass?

    BJJ is a linked system. Double Under Pass flows naturally to and from related positions. Study transitions in both directions to build a complete positional game.
