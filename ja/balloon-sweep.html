Balloon Sweep: A Precise BJJ Guide for White Belts

WHITE★★☆☆☆Intermediate
White帯テクニック
📖 完全ガイドを見る → 白帯ガイド

The Balloon Sweep is initiated from closed guard, aiming to invert your opponent and secure a sweep. White belts often struggle due to a lack of control and incorrect weight transfer.

The key insight is to use your legs as a lever, driving their weight over their head rather than trying to lift them directly.

目次

    グリップ・生体力学

    1. Establish a strong cross-collar grip with your right hand, thumb inside the lapel, and a sleeve grip with your left hand on their left sleeve.
    2. Hook your right leg's shin across the back of their left thigh, just above the knee.
    3. Hook your left leg's shin across the front of their left shin, creating a figure-four with your legs around their left leg.
    4. Drive your hips forward and to your left, angling your body slightly.
    5. Simultaneously, pull down on the collar grip and push their sleeve grip away, creating a rotational force.
    6. As they post their right hand for balance, extend your right leg sharply upwards, lifting their left leg.
    7. Maintain forward hip pressure and continue the pulling motion to unbalance them and drive their weight over their head.

    ⚠️ 白帯の注意点

    • Attempting to stand up and pull the opponent over: This can strain the lower back and knees due to excessive axial loading and torque.
    • Gripping too low on the sleeve or collar: This reduces leverage and can lead to a loss of control, potentially causing shoulder impingement when the opponent resists.
    • Not committing the hips forward: Insufficient hip drive results in pushing the opponent away rather than sweeping them, risking a failed attempt and leaving you vulnerable to passes.

    ドリル段階

    1. Solo drilling of leg positioning and hip drive without a partner (50 reps).
    2. Partner assists by holding a neutral position, focusing on grip and leg control (25% resistance, 10 reps per side).
    3. Partner offers mild resistance to the pull and push, focusing on hip movement (50% resistance, 10 reps per side).
    4. Partner actively resists the sweep attempt, requiring more commitment to the hip and leg drive (75% resistance, 5 reps per side).
    5. Live rolling with a focus on attempting the Balloon Sweep when the opportunity arises (90% resistance, 5 attempts per roll).
    6. Live rolling, prioritizing the Balloon Sweep as a primary sweep option (100% resistance, focus on completion).

    使うタイミング・カウンター

    WHEN TO ATTEMPT:

    • Opponent establishes a tight closed guard.
    • Opponent is posturing up, creating space to attack their base.
    • Opponent has a weak base and is leaning forward.

    PRIMARY COUNTERS:

    • Posture up and widen base: If the sweep is attempted, immediately widen your stance and drive your hips down to anchor yourself.
    • Drive knee into opponent's hip: As they initiate the sweep, drive your knee into their hip on the side they are sweeping towards to create a frame and prevent rotation.
    • Slice leg through: If your leg is hooked, try to slice your leg through their hook and create a strong base to prevent the sweep.

    よくある質問・トラブル

    Q: Why does my shoulder hurt when I try the Balloon Sweep?

    Your shoulder likely hurts because you are pulling too hard with your arm while your body is not properly aligned. Instead of relying solely on arm strength, focus on driving your hips forward and using your legs as levers. Ensure your collar grip is high and your sleeve grip is secure to maximize biomechanical advantage.

    Q: How can I Balloon Sweep against a much bigger and heavier opponent?

    Against a larger opponent, leverage is paramount. Focus on precise timing and the rotational aspect of the sweep. Secure a deep collar grip and use your legs to create a strong hook. Drive your hips forward with maximum commitment, using their weight against them rather than trying to lift.

    Q: When is the best time to transition to the Balloon Sweep from closed guard?

    The ideal time is when your opponent is leaning forward, attempting to pass your guard or apply pressure. This forward momentum makes them more susceptible to the rotational force of the Balloon Sweep. Avoid attempting it when they are postured high and have a strong base.
