Russian Tie — BJJ技術解説

A takedown used to bring the fight to the ground from a standing position.

𝕏 Share Reddit 📍 Track Progress → 🌳 Skill Tree

⚙️ ステップバイステップ

1 Establish Grips

Control the collar, sleeve, or underhook to set up the throw.

2 Break Posture

Pull or push to break their stance and create movement.

3 Execute the Technique

Use hip drive, leg sweep, or shoulder throw to take them down.

4 Land and Control

Land in a dominant ground position and establish control immediately.
