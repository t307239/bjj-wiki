Rubber Guard Technique for BJJ White Belts

PURPLE★★★★☆Advanced
Purple帯テクニック
📖 完全ガイドを見る → 紫帯昇格要件

Rubber Guard starts from closed guard and aims to control the opponent's posture and limbs for submissions or sweeps.

White belts often rush, forgetting the importance of posture control and hip engagement.

The core principle is using your shin across their shoulder to break their posture and create leverage.

目次

    グリップ・生体力学

    1. Opponent is in your closed guard.
    2. Secure a deep cross-collar grip with your right hand, pulling their head towards your chest.
    3. Use your left hand to grip their left sleeve, near the wrist.
    4. Swing your right leg up and over their left shoulder, ensuring your shin is flat across their neck and shoulder line.
    5. Your right foot hooks behind your left knee, creating a tight triangle with your legs.
    6. Rotate your hips to the left, driving your right knee towards the mat.
    7. Maintain a strong frame with your left arm, pushing against their right shoulder to prevent them from posturing up.

    ⚠️ 白帯の注意点

    • Attempting to lift the opponent by pulling their collar with both hands: This can hyperextend your own elbows, risking ulnar collateral ligament tears.
    • Letting your leg drop below the opponent's shoulder line: This creates a gap, allowing them to posture up and potentially injure your knee by driving into it.
    • Forgetting to connect your right foot to your left knee: This makes the leg ineffective, allowing the opponent to easily remove your leg and pass your guard.

    ドリル段階

    1. Solo: Practice bringing your leg over the shoulder and hooking your foot 10 times per side.
    2. Solo: Practice the hip rotation and frame with no opponent, 10 reps.
    3. Partner Drill (0% resistance): Partner stands passively. Execute the full Rubber Guard setup 5 times.
    4. Partner Drill (25% resistance): Partner offers light resistance to your leg placement. Execute 5 times.
    5. Partner Drill (50% resistance): Partner attempts to break posture. Execute the setup and hold for 30 seconds, 3 times.
    6. Light Rolling (75% resistance): Attempt to establish Rubber Guard from closed guard for 1 minute.

    使うタイミング・カウンター

    WHEN TO ATTEMPT:

    • When the opponent is posturing down towards you in closed guard.
    • When the opponent attempts to stack pass from closed guard.
    • When you have secured a strong cross-collar grip and can control their head.

    PRIMARY COUNTERS:

    • Opponent stacks: Drive your hips forward and away, using your leg on their shoulder to create space and potentially sweep.
    • Opponent defends by grabbing your leg: Use your free hand to push their attacking arm away and re-establish your frame.
    • Opponent tries to pass by stepping around: Keep your hips tight and use your leg on their shoulder to control their movement, preventing them from creating distance.

    よくある質問・トラブル

    Q: Why does my knee hurt when I try to set up Rubber Guard?

    Your knee might hurt if you are not properly rotating your hips and instead are forcing the leg over the shoulder. Ensure your shin is flat across their neck and shoulder, and your right foot hooks behind your left knee. This creates a stable frame, preventing hyperextension or excessive torque on the knee joint.

    Q: How can I use Rubber Guard effectively against a much bigger opponent?

    Against a larger opponent, leverage is key. Focus on breaking their posture immediately with a strong cross-collar grip and driving your shin across their shoulder. Your hips must be active, creating angles and preventing them from using their weight advantage to crush you. Use your free hand to maintain a strong frame on their bicep or shoulder.

    Q: Is it okay to keep my leg in Rubber Guard for a long time?

    While Rubber Guard offers control, prolonged static positions can be inefficient. Your goal is to use the control to set up submissions or sweeps. Continuously adjust your hip angle and use your controlling leg to manipulate their posture. Transitioning quickly to a submission like a triangle choke or omoplata is more effective than holding the guard statically.
