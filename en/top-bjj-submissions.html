Top 10 BJJ Submissions Every Practitioner Must Know

🥋 Blue Belt ★★☆☆☆ Intermediate

Whether you're a white belt or preparing for your first competition, knowing the most reliable submission techniques is essential. These 10 techniques account for the majority of tap-outs at all belt levels.

The List

#1

Rear Naked Choke

The highest percentage submission in grappling. Applied from back mount, it works at every belt level.

→ #2

Armbar

The most versatile submission in BJJ — attackable from guard, mount, and back positions.

→ #3

Triangle Choke

A blood choke requiring minimal strength. Perfectly suited for smaller grapplers.

→ #4

Guillotine Choke

High-percentage from stand-up and guard. One of the fastest-finishing submissions.

→ #5

Kimura

Shoulder lock with extreme versatility. Also a powerful position control tool.

→ #6

Heel Hook

The most powerful leg lock. Devastating at advanced levels — must be learned with care.

→ #7

D'Arce Choke

A no-gi favorite that finishes fast when set up correctly from turtle or side control.

→ #8

Bow and Arrow Choke

The highest percentage gi choke from back mount. Simple mechanics, powerful finish.

→ #9

Omoplata

Shoulder lock from guard that doubles as a sweep threat. A true Swiss Army knife technique.

→ #10

Ankle Lock

Legal at all levels, the ankle lock is the entry point to leg lock attacks.

→

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the highest percentage BJJ submission?

The Rear Naked Choke (RNC) is statistically the most finished submission in both gi and no-gi competition. Its simple mechanics and dominant positional requirement (back mount) make it extremely reliable.

Q: Which submission should beginners learn first?

The armbar from closed guard is ideal for beginners — it teaches positional awareness, hip movement, and attack timing simultaneously. The rear naked choke is equally important.

Q: Are leg locks safe for beginners?

Straight ankle locks are safe and legal at white belt in most organizations. Heel hooks require more experience and positional awareness before adding to your game.
