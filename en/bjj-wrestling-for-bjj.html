Why Wrestling Improves Your BJJ

Wrestling adds explosive takedown offense, elite sprawl defense and scramble awareness that BJJ training alone rarely develops. Every high-level no-gi grappler has significant wrestling in their game.

Wrestling Drill Recommendations for BJJ

  • Shot drill: Level change + penetration step × 20 reps each side daily
  • Sprawl drill: Partner shoots, you sprawl and establish front headlock × 10
  • Scramble drill: Both start in referee position, one attacks, work for dominant position
💡 Pro Tip: Even 2-3 wrestling classes per month will significantly improve your standup BJJ. Focus on the level change and penetration step — these transfer the most.

Frequently Asked Questions

What wrestling skills are most important for BJJ?
Level change and penetration step for shots, sprawl for takedown defense, and scramble awareness. The ability to fight for underhooks and understand positional wrestling is also critical.
How does the sprawl work in BJJ?
When someone shoots, shoot your hips back and down while driving your chest into their upper back. This removes your legs from their grasp. From the sprawl, you can work toward the back or a front headlock.
What is a scramble in BJJ?
A scramble is a transitional phase where both grapplers are fighting for position without either having clear control. Wrestling training improves scramble awareness — knowing when to take risks and when to disengage.
