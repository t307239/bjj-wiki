    ❓ FAQ

    Do BJJ knee pads actually help?
    Yes — compression sleeves improve blood flow, reduce swelling post-training, and give proprioceptive feedback that helps protect against awkward twisting.
    Should I wear knee pads during guard work?
    Absolutely. Open guard players who frequently shoot their legs put significant stress on knee ligaments and cartilage.
    What's the difference between a knee pad and knee sleeve?
    Knee sleeves provide compression/warmth. Knee pads have hard shell padding for impact. For BJJ, compression sleeves are most useful.

    Using Strength Over Technique

    Skipping Drilling

    Techniques only become available in live rolling after extensive drilling. Regular repetition builds the muscle memory needed for execution under pressure.

    Ignoring Defensive Reactions

    Every technique has common counters. Learn the most frequent defensive reactions and have follow-up attacks ready.

    More Questions

    What are the main benefits of wearing knee pads in BJJ?

    Knee pads offer crucial protection against mat burn, impacts, and pressure from opponents' knees. They also provide a bit of extra cushioning during certain drills and techniques, improving comfort and reducing the risk of minor injuries.

    Are BJJ knee pads mandatory for beginners?

    While not strictly mandatory in most academies, wearing knee pads is highly recommended for beginners. They help prevent common discomforts like mat burn and offer a layer of protection as you learn to navigate positions and movements.

    How do I choose the right size BJJ knee pads?

    Knee pads should fit snugly but not be so tight that they restrict blood flow or movement. Measure the circumference of your knee and thigh according to the manufacturer's sizing chart to ensure a proper, comfortable fit that stays in place during training.
