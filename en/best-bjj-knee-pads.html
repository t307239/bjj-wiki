❓ FAQ

Do BJJ knee pads actually help?
Yes — compression sleeves improve blood flow, reduce swelling post-training, and give proprioceptive feedback that helps protect against awkward twisting.
Should I wear knee pads during guard work?
Absolutely. Open guard players who frequently shoot their legs put significant stress on knee ligaments and cartilage.
What's the difference between a knee pad and knee sleeve?
Knee sleeves provide compression/warmth. Knee pads have hard shell padding for impact. For BJJ, compression sleeves are most useful.
