Lachlan Giles, nicknamed 'The Australian Heel Hook King', is one of the most accomplished Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu athletes from Australian. Known for exceptional skill in heel hook, inside heel hook, 50 50 guard, leg entanglement, Lachlan has earned a reputation as one of the sport's most technically refined competitors. Training out of Absolute MMA (Melbourne), they have amassed an impressive collection of world titles and competition victories across gi and no-gi formats.

Beyond competition, Lachlan Giles has contributed to the BJJ community through teaching, instructionals, and demonstrating that technical mastery can prevail over physical advantages. Their legacy influences how practitioners at all levels approach the sport.



Today, Lachlan Giles continues to train, teach, and compete, passing on the lessons of their championship career to the next generation of BJJ athletes.
