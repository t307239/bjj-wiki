Lachlan Giles

BJJ AthleteADCC MedalistBlack Belt

Lachlan Giles is an Australian BJJ black belt, ADCC 2019 bronze medalist, leg lock specialist, and founder of Absolute MMA in Melbourne, Australia. He gained worldwide recognition at the 2019 ADCC World Championships, where — despite competing below his natural weight class — he submitted three of the world's top grapplers in the absolute division, including Patrick Gaudio and Kaynan Duarte, showcasing a leg lock system of remarkable precision and depth. He is also a highly regarded instructor whose online content has influenced thousands of grapplers worldwide.

Biography

Lachlan Giles grew up in Australia and built his BJJ career at Absolute MMA in Melbourne, which he co-founded and continues to run as head coach. He trained in both gi and no-gi competition, earning his black belt and developing a distinctive leg lock game that became his competitive signature.

Giles competed regularly at IBJJF and ADCC events, but his defining competitive moment came at the 2019 ADCC World Championships in Anaheim, California — one of the most celebrated performances in modern grappling history.

Competitive Style

Lachlan Giles's game is built around:

  • Leg lock system: His primary weapon — a deep, systematic approach to leg entanglements, heel hooks (inside and outside), kneebars, and toe holds.
  • Half guard and quarter guard entries: Many of Giles's leg lock entries come from guard positions, particularly quarter guard transitions to ashi garami.
  • Back attacks: Giles developed a comprehensive back attack system that he has detailed in instructional content — using the back take as a complement to his leg lock game.
  • Guard passing: Technical and systematic passing, often using pressure and leg drag sequences.
  • Mental composure: One of Giles's most noted qualities is his ability to execute technical precision under pressure in high-stakes competition.

Leg Lock System

Giles's leg lock system is one of the most detailed and systematically presented in modern BJJ instruction. Core elements:

  • Ashi garami positions: Systematic entry and control in single-leg ashi, outside ashi (411/saddle), and outside heel hook positions.
  • Inside heel hook: His most reliable finish — rotating the heel with proper hip mechanics for maximum efficiency and minimum energy.
  • Outside heel hook: From the 411 position — requires proper control to prevent the opponent from spinning out.
  • Kneebar: Applied when opponents straighten their leg in defense of heel hooks.
  • Entry chains: Giles emphasizes transitioning between leg lock positions when the initial attack is defended, creating a "system" rather than isolated attacks.

ADCC 2019 Performance

At the 2019 ADCC World Championships in Anaheim, Lachlan Giles entered the absolute division (open weight) while normally competing at under 88kg — making him one of the smaller competitors in the absolute field. His performance is considered one of the greatest in modern grappling:

  • Submitted Patrick Gaudio (World Champion, +99kg) via heel hook in overtime
  • Submitted Kaynan Duarte (World Champion, +99kg) via heel hook in overtime
  • Submitted Roberto "Cyborg" Abreu (3x ADCC Champion) via heel hook
  • Won the bronze medal in the absolute division

The performance — a smaller competitor heel-hooking three of the biggest names in grappling — was broadcast worldwide and dramatically raised Giles's international profile and the legitimacy of leg locks as absolute-division weapons.

Instructional Work

Lachlan Giles has created some of the most acclaimed instructional content in BJJ, including his "High Percentage Leg Locks" and "High Percentage Back Attacks" series, widely considered definitive references for these positions. His teaching style is analytical, systematic, and accessible — he breaks down complex leg lock and back attack concepts into learnable components, making advanced techniques approachable for practitioners at all levels.

Legacy

Lachlan Giles's legacy is his contribution to leg lock education and the 2019 ADCC absolute performance that became a defining moment in the public consciousness around submission grappling. He is one of the primary figures responsible for making systematic leg lock study accessible to everyday BJJ practitioners worldwide through his instructionals and online content.

⚡ Learning from Lachlan Giles

  • Study his "High Percentage Leg Locks" instructional — it's widely considered the most systematic leg lock curriculum available.
  • His entry system from quarter guard and half guard is directly applicable at any belt level in no-gi.
  • Watch the 2019 ADCC absolute footage — Giles's composure and execution under pressure against much larger opponents is instructive beyond just technique.
  • His back attack system is a natural complement to leg locks — studying both together reveals how they create a complete submission game.
Q: What is Lachlan Giles most famous for?

Lachlan Giles is most famous for his 2019 ADCC absolute performance, where he submitted Patrick Gaudio, Kaynan Duarte, and Roberto "Cyborg" Abreu (all world-class heavyweights) via heel hooks to win the bronze medal. He is also highly regarded for his instructional content on leg locks and back attacks.

Q: Where is Lachlan Giles based?

Lachlan Giles is based in Melbourne, Australia, where he coaches at Absolute MMA — the academy he co-founded and continues to run as head coach.
