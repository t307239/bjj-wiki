    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: Why do I keep getting swept easily when I'm trying to stand up in BJJ and how can I improve my balance?

    Your base is likely too narrow, making you unstable. To improve, widen your stance by placing your feet shoulder-width apart and slightly staggered, creating a larger, more stable foundation. Keep your weight distributed evenly over the balls of your feet, allowing for quick adjustments to maintain your center of gravity over your base.

    Q: How can I stop bigger opponents from unbalancing me when I'm in their guard and trying to pass?

    When facing a larger opponent, focus on maintaining a low center of gravity by bending your knees deeply and keeping your hips close to the mat. This forces them to generate more force to move you, and you can use your core and leg strength to anchor yourself. Distribute your weight through your feet and knees, not just your hands, to absorb their pressure and prevent them from leveraging your limbs.

    Q: What are the best drills to improve my ability to stay on my feet when someone is trying to pull me down in BJJ?

    Practice 'wizards' and 'shrimping' drills to develop dynamic balance. For wizards, imagine someone is trying to sweep you and use your arms to create space and brace against their hips, keeping your base wide and low. Shrimping involves explosively pushing your hips away from pressure while maintaining a low base, teaching you to quickly re-establish your center of gravity.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Why is balance so important in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

    Balance is foundational in BJJ because it dictates your ability to control your opponent and resist their attempts to control you. Without good balance, you'll be easily swept, taken down, or have your submissions countered.

    How can I improve my balance off the mat?

    You can improve your static balance by practicing standing on one leg for extended periods, or by using balance boards and stability balls. Dynamic balance can be trained through activities like yoga or simply by practicing controlled movements and shifts in weight.

    What are some common balance drills for BJJ beginners?

    Beginners can practice drills like maintaining a stable base while a partner gently pushes them, or practicing controlled falling and recovery. Simply drilling fundamental movements like shrimping and bridging with a focus on staying grounded are also excellent.
