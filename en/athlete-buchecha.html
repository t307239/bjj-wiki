Buchecha

BJJ Athlete13x World ChampionBlack Belt

Marcus Almeida, known worldwide as "Buchecha", is a Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu black belt widely considered the greatest heavyweight competitor of all time and one of the top three greatest BJJ competitors ever. A student of the Caio Terra and Alliance system, Buchecha won an unprecedented 13 IBJJF World Championship titles — 11 black belt world titles at ultra-heavyweight and absolute — while being virtually unstoppable in gi BJJ for over a decade. He later transitioned to MMA, where he continues competing.

Biography

Born in 1991 in Manhuaçu, Minas Gerais, Brazil, Marcus Almeida moved to São Paulo and began training BJJ. He trained under Caio Terra before joining Alliance, where he trained under Alexandre "Gigi" Paiva. His nickname "Buchecha" (Brazilian slang for chubby cheeks) was given in childhood and stuck throughout his legendary career.

Buchecha turned professional in BJJ and rapidly became the dominant force in the ultra-heavyweight division. His combination of size, technique, and athleticism was historically unmatched — most ultra-heavyweight champions relied on power, but Buchecha brought a technical game that could compete with the best at any weight class.

After dominating gi BJJ, Buchecha transitioned to MMA competition, signing with ONE Championship, where he continued to demonstrate elite grappling at the highest levels.

Competitive Style

Buchecha's game combined:

  • Explosive guard passing: Buchecha passed guard with both power and technique — torreando, knee slice, and pressure-based smash passes in his arsenal.
  • Back taking: Buchecha was one of the most relentless back-takers in BJJ. He pursued the back from almost every position and was extremely dangerous with the rear naked choke.
  • Athletic guard play: Despite competing at ultra-heavyweight, Buchecha played a legitimate guard — X-guard sweeps, single-leg takedowns, and dynamic entries.
  • Submission finishing: Unlike many points-focused heavyweights, Buchecha finished matches regularly with chokes, armbars, and back attacks.

Guard Passing

Buchecha's guard passing was physically dominant but also technically refined. His passing incorporated:

  • Smash passing: Using his weight and pressure to flatten opponents and create chest-to-chest pressure, then transitioning to side control or north-south.
  • Torreando: Fast, redirecting passes that leveraged his long arms and explosive hip movement.
  • Knee slice: Precise and powerful, often combined with bodyweight pressure to prevent recovery.

Back Attack System

Buchecha's back taking and finishing from back control was his most lethal weapon. He consistently took opponents' backs through:

  • Passing guard → opponent gives up the turtle → back take
  • Scrambles from guard → early back exposure → back take
  • Failed submission defense → opponent turns → back take

Once on the back, Buchecha's rear naked choke mechanics were textbook — patient, methodical seatbelt control, breaking down defensive grips, and applying the choke with compound precision.

13 World Titles

Buchecha won 13 IBJJF World Championship titles: brown belt titles, then 11 black belt titles including multiple ultra-heavyweight and absolute titles from 2011 through 2018. He is the most decorated BJJ World Champion in history at the time of writing. His absolute titles — where he competed against all weight classes — are particularly remarkable given that he was already competing at the heaviest weight class.

MMA Career

Buchecha signed with ONE Championship in 2021 and began his MMA career, winning his first several bouts, including a notable victory over Rodrigo "Minotauro" Nogueira. His grappling remains elite in MMA context, and his BJJ credentials make him one of the most technically skilled grapplers ever to enter the cage.

Legacy

Buchecha's legacy is defined by longevity, dominance, and technical evolution. He is the measuring stick for heavyweight BJJ — any competitor claiming greatness at the heavier weights is compared to his record. His transition to MMA opened a new chapter, but his BJJ achievements remain unmatched at ultra-heavyweight.

⚡ Learning from Buchecha

  • Study his back-taking sequences — he finds the back from virtually every position, always seeking the shortest path.
  • His guard passing shows that pressure passing and speed passing are not mutually exclusive — great passers blend both.
  • Watch how he hunts submissions from back control — he never rushes, always consolidates position before attacking.
  • His guard game at heavyweight proves that technical guard play scales to all body types when the fundamentals are solid.
Q: How many World Championships did Buchecha win?

Marcus "Buchecha" Almeida won 13 IBJJF World Championships total — including brown belt titles and 11 black belt titles at ultra-heavyweight and absolute. He is the most decorated BJJ World Champion in the sport's history.

Q: Is Buchecha competing in MMA?

Yes. Buchecha transitioned to MMA and signed with ONE Championship in 2021. He has competed and won in MMA while his BJJ skills continue to be a foundation of his fighting game.
