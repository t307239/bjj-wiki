    Biography

    Ffion Davies is the greatest female no-gi grappler of her era. The Welshwoman shocked the world by defeating Beatriz Mesquita at ADCC 2022 to claim the World Championship. Known for her relentless leg drag passing and leg lock combinations, Davies is a complete technical grappler who continues to dominate no-gi competition globally.

    "Every time I step on the mat, I want to be the best version of myself."
    — Ffion Davies
