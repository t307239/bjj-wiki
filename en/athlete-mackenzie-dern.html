    Biography

    Mackenzie Dern is the daughter of BJJ legend Wellington 'Megaton' Dias and grew up on the mats. She won multiple World Championships in both gi and no-gi before transitioning to UFC where she has become one of the most technical grapplers in MMA. Known for her relentless submission hunting from every position.

    "BJJ is in my DNA. I grew up on the mats and I will die on the mats."
    — Mackenzie Dern
