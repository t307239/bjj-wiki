Best BJJ Guard Positions: Complete Guide for All Levels

Your guard is your primary defensive and offensive tool when playing off your back. Choosing the right guard system can transform your entire BJJ game. Here are the most effective guards across all experience levels.

The List

#1

Closed Guard

The foundation of BJJ. Full hip control with endless attack options — the perfect guard to master first.

→ #2

Half Guard

One of the most practical guards for all sizes. Excellent sweeps, easy to retain, and effective in self-defense.

→ #3

Butterfly Guard

Explosive sweeps with both legs as hooks. Exceptional for smaller and more athletic players.

→ #4

De La Riva Guard

The backbone of modern sport BJJ. Ideal counter to toreando and leg drag passing attempts.

→ #5

X-Guard

Marcelo Garcia's signature — arguably the most powerful sweep position in BJJ.

→ #6

Spider Guard

Lasso-like sleeve grips create maximum distance and leverage against guard passers.

→ #7

Deep Half Guard

An underrated system with unique sweep options that surprises opponents at all levels.

→ #8

Rubber Guard

Eddie Bravo's high guard system — requires flexibility but creates tight submission chains.

→ #9

50/50 Guard

Neutral leg entanglement system key to modern leg lock exchanges. Mandatory in no-gi.

→ #10

Lasso Guard

Gi-only weapon that creates extreme breaking power for sweeps and submissions.

→

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the best guard for beginners?

Closed guard and half guard are the best starting points. Closed guard teaches fundamental concepts of control and attack, while half guard is practical in sparring from the start.

Q: What guard is best for self-defense?

Closed guard and rubber guard are the most practical for self-defense scenarios — they control distance and prevent strikes effectively.

Q: Which guard works best in no-gi?

Butterfly guard, X-guard, and 50/50 are the most effective no-gi guard positions since they don't rely on gi grips.
