💪 BJJ Strength Training Guide 2026

You don't need to be the strongest person on the mat — but purposeful strength training makes everything easier. Here's the evidence-based approach used by elite BJJ competitors.

Key Principle: Strength training for BJJ should improve mat performance, not create fatigue that hurts your technique work.

🏋️ The Big 4 Lifts for BJJ

Deadlift

Builds total-body pulling strength. Directly translates to guard passing and takedown defence.

Romanian Deadlift

Hip hinge strength for guard retention and hip escape explosiveness.

Pull-Ups / Rows

Upper back strength for clinch control and guard breaking.

Zercher Squat

Simulates carrying someone's weight — essential for wrestling and guard passing.

❓ FAQ

How many days per week should BJJ athletes lift?

2–3 days is optimal for most BJJ athletes. More can interfere with technique development and recovery.

Should I strength train before or after BJJ?

On separate days when possible. If same-day, BJJ first (technique requires fresh neuromuscular capacity), strength second.

What's the best rep range for BJJ strength?

Mix heavy (3–5 reps) for max strength and moderate (8–12 reps) for hypertrophy. Avoid grinding sets that destroy recovery.
