BJJ Gi Grip Fundamentals: Core Gripping Techniques

Updated March 2025 • 7 min read

Contents

    The Importance of Grip

    In gi jiu-jitsu, grips are everything. Your grip quality directly affects your ability to control your opponent, set up submissions, and defend attacks. Strong fundamentals in gripping create the foundation for all technical skills.

    Core Grip Types

    Lapel Grips

    Lapel grips involve controlling the lapel of your opponent's gi. The lapel is one of the most versatile parts of the gi, enabling chokes, sweeps, and positional control. Learn proper hand placement and grip strength for lapel control.

    Sleeve Grips

    Sleeve grips provide excellent control of arm movement and enable many sweeping and passing techniques. A strong sleeve grip limits your opponent's options significantly.

    Collar Grips

    The collar offers opportunities for chokes and pressure. Understanding collar grip variations gives you access to powerful finishing techniques.

    Grip Placement Principles

    Hand Position

    Proper hand position determines grip effectiveness. Your thumb placement, finger position, and palm engagement all matter. Develop awareness of how small adjustments improve grip security.

    Grip Depth

    Grip depth affects control. Deep grips provide more security but less range, while shallower grips allow more movement. Learn when each is appropriate.

    Building Grip Strength

    Strong grip fundamentals require:

    • Regular grip training and drilling
    • Proper finger and forearm conditioning
    • Specific grip strength exercises
    • Live rolling practice

    Common Grip Transitions

    Learning smooth transitions between grip types multiplies the effectiveness of your game. Practice transitioning from lapel to sleeve to collar grips fluidly.

    Pro Tip: Don't grip tightly from the start. Develop fine control and sensitivity in your grip pressure. The best grapplers use just enough grip strength to maintain control while conserving energy.
