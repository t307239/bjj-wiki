    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: Why can't I generate enough power to lift my opponent with the BJJ Hip Bump Sweep, even when I push hard?

    The power comes from your hips, not your arms. You need to drive your hips up and into your opponent by extending your legs and arching your back, creating a fulcrum with your hip bone. Think of it as a lever action, where your hip is the pivot point and your body weight is the force.

    Q: My opponent just posts their arm on my hip and stops my BJJ Hip Bump Sweep, what can I do?

    Instead of pushing directly into their posted arm, angle your hips slightly away from it and drive upwards. This shifts the point of contact, allowing you to use your body weight to 'roll' over their arm rather than trying to force through it. Simultaneously, use your free arm to push their shoulder or head away to create space and momentum.

    Q: How can I make sure I don't get my arm trapped or swept myself when attempting the BJJ Hip Bump Sweep?

    Keep your framing arm actively engaged by pushing into their hip or thigh to maintain distance and control. As you initiate the hip bump, ensure your base leg remains planted and drives into the mat, preventing them from easily bridging or turning into you. Your hips should move in a controlled, upward motion, not a wild flail.

    More Questions

    How do I generate enough power for the hip bump sweep?

    The power comes from a strong hip extension, almost like bridging off the mat. Ensure your hips are loaded and you drive them upwards and slightly towards your opponent's shoulders.

    My opponent just posts their arm and stops the sweep, what do I do?

    If they post an arm, you can use that arm as a lever. Instead of pushing straight up, try to push their posted arm down and away as you drive your hips, or transition to a different sweep.

    What if my opponent turns into me instead of away?

    If they turn into you, you can use that momentum to transition to a guard pass or a submission. Look to secure a dominant side control or armbar opportunity as they commit their weight.
