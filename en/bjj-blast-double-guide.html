    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does it take to learn Blast Double Guide?

    Most practitioners develop functional competency with Blast Double Guide within 3–6 months of consistent drilling. Mastery — the ability to execute reliably in live rolling against resisting opponents — typically takes 1–2 years.

    Is Blast Double Guide effective for beginners?

    Yes. Blast Double Guide is part of the core BJJ curriculum and taught at all belt levels. Beginners should focus on the fundamental mechanics and concepts before refining advanced entries.

    How often should I drill Blast Double Guide?

    3–5 times per week is ideal for rapid skill acquisition. Even 10 focused repetitions per session compounds over time — consistency matters more than volume.

    What positions connect to Blast Double Guide?

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: How can I make sure I'm driving with my legs and not just my arms when I attempt a BJJ blast double leg takedown?

    To drive with your legs, initiate the takedown by dropping your hips low and driving forward with your glutes and hamstrings. Keep your chest up and your shoulders driving into your opponent's hips, using your legs as the primary engine for forward momentum and elevation.

    Q: What's the correct head position to avoid getting my head smashed or injured during a BJJ blast double leg?

    When executing the blast double leg, your head should be on the outside of your opponent's hips, typically on the same side as your lead leg, and driving forward into their hip bone. This position protects your head and neck while allowing you to generate power and control their balance.

    Q: How do I maintain a strong base and prevent my opponent from easily sprawling when I go for a BJJ blast double leg?

    To maintain base and counter the sprawl, keep your hips low and drive forward aggressively, ensuring your shoulders are planted firmly into your opponent's hips. Simultaneously, maintain a tight grip on their legs and step your back leg through to secure the takedown, preventing them from creating space to sprawl effectively.

    More Questions

    What is the main goal of a blast double leg takedown in BJJ?

    The primary goal is to quickly close the distance and secure a takedown by attacking both of your opponent's legs simultaneously. This aims to disrupt their balance and bring them to the mat.

    How do I finish a blast double leg if my opponent sprawls?

    If they sprawl, you need to maintain your grip and drive through their hips, trying to lift their legs or drive them forward. You can also transition to a single leg if the double leg is completely stuffed.

    What are the common mistakes beginners make with the blast double leg?

    Common mistakes include not committing fully, attacking too far away, and not driving through the opponent's hips after securing the legs. Failing to maintain a strong base and posture can also lead to failure.
