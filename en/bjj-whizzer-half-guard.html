    More Questions

    What is a whizzer in BJJ?

    A whizzer is a powerful overhook grip applied by the person on top, typically in guard retention or passing situations. It's used to control the opponent's arm and hip, preventing them from creating space or reversing position.

    How do I use the whizzer effectively in half guard?

    When you're in half guard and your opponent tries to pass, you can secure a whizzer by reaching over their back and gripping their far armpit or shoulder. This grip helps you maintain your base, prevent them from flattening you out, and can be used to set up sweeps or maintain top control.

    What are common mistakes when using the whizzer in half guard?

    A common mistake is not committing to the grip, allowing the opponent to easily break it. Another mistake is relying solely on the whizzer without proper body positioning, leaving you vulnerable to sweeps or submissions.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: Why does my shoulder feel like it's popping out when I try to whizzer in half guard?

    This often happens when you're using your arm and shoulder muscles to create pressure instead of your lats and core. To fix this, drive your elbow down and back, engaging your latissimus dorsi muscle, and keep your shoulder blade retracted to protect the joint.

    Q: How can I effectively whizzer in half guard to stop my opponent from passing my guard, especially when they are much bigger than me?

    Against a larger opponent, focus on using your hips and core to create a strong base rather than just arm strength. Drive your hips into their hip, anchoring your whizzer arm deep under their armpit with your bicep tight to their side, using your entire body to create leverage.

    Q: What's the proper way to position my body when I whizzer in half guard to prevent them from getting side control?

    To prevent side control, maintain a strong connection by keeping your chest tight to their chest and your hips active. Your whizzer arm should be positioned to prevent their hips from turning into you, while your free arm can hook their far leg or hip to maintain distance and control their base.
