🎎 BJJ Gi Etiquette

BJJ etiquette preserves the culture of the art, keeps training safe, and creates the mutual respect that makes the gym a place where everyone improves. Many rules are unwritten — here are the most important ones.

Core Etiquette Rules

RuleWhy It Matters
Tap early, tap oftenProtects you and your partner from injury
No shoes on the matPrevents bacteria and mat contamination
Trim fingernails and toenailsPrevents cuts and scratches on partners
Wash gi after every sessionHygiene — essential for shared mat environments
Bow when entering/exiting matTraditional respect for the training space
Don't coach during sparringDisrupts partner's learning process

Unwritten Mat Rules

SituationEtiquette
Asking to rollNod or extend hand — never demand
Rolling with higher beltsDon't slam them; let technique flow
Rolling with lower beltsDon't crush — help them learn
After a submissionReset with respect, don't celebrate excessively
Injury during rollingStop immediately, check on partner
Pro Tip: Tapping is not defeat — it's smart training. Athletes who refuse to tap suffer injuries that cost months of mat time. Honor your partner's technique by tapping cleanly.
