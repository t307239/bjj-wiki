    More Questions

    How do I prevent my opponent from passing my spider guard in no-gi?

    In no-gi spider guard, grip fighting is paramount. Focus on controlling your opponent's wrists or biceps to maintain distance and prevent them from establishing a strong base to pass. Using your feet to push off their hips or shoulders can also create space and disrupt their balance.

    What are the primary attacking options from no-gi spider guard?

    Common attacks include triangle chokes, omoplatas, and sweeps like the 'helicopter' sweep or a simple leg drag sweep. The key is to use your foot on their hip or bicep to create an angle and then transition into your submission or sweep attempt.

    How do I maintain control and avoid getting my feet tangled in no-gi spider guard?

    Active foot placement and constant pressure are crucial. Instead of just resting your feet, use them to actively push, pull, and control your opponent's limbs and hips. Keep your legs slightly bent and your core engaged to maintain a strong and flexible guard.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: When I try to use Spider Guard in No-Gi, my opponent just walks through my legs, what am I doing wrong with my arm positioning?

    In No-Gi Spider Guard, your grips should be on the opponent's biceps or triceps, not the collar. Maintain tension by extending your arms and pulling their sleeves towards your hips, creating a strong frame that prevents them from easily closing the distance.

    Q: How can I effectively sweep an opponent who is much heavier than me when I'm in No-Gi Spider Guard?

    To sweep a heavier opponent from No-Gi Spider Guard, focus on off-balancing them by using your leg hooks to pull their weight towards you while simultaneously creating an angle with your hips. The key is to use their weight against them by extending your legs and hip escaping to generate momentum for the sweep.

    Q: My opponent keeps posturing up and passing my No-Gi Spider Guard easily, what should I do with my legs to keep them controlled?

    To prevent posture in No-Gi Spider Guard, actively use your feet to control their biceps or forearms, preventing them from straightening their arms. By keeping your legs bent and actively 'walking' your feet up their arms, you can maintain a tight connection and limit their ability to posture effectively.
