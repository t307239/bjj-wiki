    More Questions

    How do I get stripes on my white belt?

    Stripes are typically awarded by your instructor to signify progress in learning fundamental techniques and demonstrating consistent attendance. They are a visual representation of your journey through the white belt phase before earning your blue belt.

    Are stripes the same as interim grades?

    Yes, in many academies, 'stripes' and 'interim grades' are used interchangeably. They both refer to the small markings on your belt that indicate you are progressing towards the next belt color.

    How long does it take to get stripes on a white belt?

    The time it takes to earn stripes varies greatly depending on the individual's learning pace, dedication, and the instructor's criteria. Consistent training, demonstrating understanding of techniques, and showing good mat etiquette are key factors.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: As a brand new white belt, I'm struggling to understand the difference between getting a stripe and what feels like a temporary 'interim grade' before a real belt promotion, can you explain the purpose of these smaller achievements in BJJ?

    Stripes on your white belt signify progress in mastering fundamental techniques and demonstrating a growing understanding of BJJ principles. Interim grades, often seen in some academies, might represent a broader assessment of your overall development, including sparring ability and consistency, before a full belt promotion is considered.

    Q: I've heard instructors mention 'interim grades' and 'stripes' for white belts, but I'm confused about how to earn them. What specific actions or knowledge should I be focusing on to get my first stripe or achieve that interim grade?

    To earn stripes and progress towards interim grades, focus on consistently demonstrating correct execution of core techniques like the shrimp escape and basic guard retention, maintaining proper posture, and actively participating in positional sparring with a focus on control and understanding rather than just winning.

    Q: My instructor mentioned I'm close to an 'interim grade' but still need more stripes. What's the difference in what they're looking for when awarding a stripe versus this interim grade for a white belt?

    Stripes typically reward proficiency in specific, individual techniques or a small set of related movements, demonstrating technical competence. An interim grade, on the other hand, often reflects a more holistic evaluation of your overall Jiu-Jitsu journey, encompassing your ability to apply techniques under pressure, your mat etiquette, and your commitment to consistent training over a longer period.
