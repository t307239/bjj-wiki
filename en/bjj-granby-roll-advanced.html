    The Granby Roll Explained

    The Granby roll is an advanced escape technique where you roll over your shoulder, using your head and shoulder to create a fulcrum for the reversal. This roll is highly effective against pressure when executed with proper technique.

    Proper Positioning

    Initial Setup

    Start from turtle position with your opponent applying top pressure. Your weight distribution is key. Position yourself so that rolling over one shoulder is possible.

    Head Placement

    Your head position determines the roll's effectiveness. Place your forehead or the crown of your head on the ground as the pivot point for the roll.

    Execution Mechanics

    The Roll Direction

    Commit completely to the roll direction. A hesitant Granby roll fails. You must be explosive and committed to the movement for it to work.

    Shoulder Engagement

    Use your shoulders actively in the roll. Roll across your shoulders, not just your head. Your shoulder muscles help drive the reversal.

    Advanced Variations

    Dynamic Granby Roll

    The advanced version involves less reliance on your head and more on full-body momentum. This version works better against experienced opponents who are ready for the standard Granby.

    Chaining Granby Rolls

    Link multiple rolls together. When the first roll doesn't fully reverse the position, immediately transition to another roll or escape.

    When Granby Rolls Work Best

    • Against opponents applying heavy downward pressure
    • When your opponent is committed to back control
    • In situations where standard escapes are being defended
    • Against less mobile top opponents
    Pro Tip: The Granby roll is risk/reward. When it works, it works spectacularly. But if it fails, you might give up an even worse position. Train this technique extensively before using it in competition.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does it take to learn Granby Roll Advanced?

    Most practitioners develop functional competency with Granby Roll Advanced within 3–6 months of consistent drilling. Mastery — the ability to execute reliably in live rolling against resisting opponents — typically takes 1–2 years.

    Is Granby Roll Advanced effective for beginners?

    Yes. Granby Roll Advanced is part of the core BJJ curriculum and taught at all belt levels. Beginners should focus on the fundamental mechanics and concepts before refining advanced entries.

    How often should I drill Granby Roll Advanced?

    3–5 times per week is ideal for rapid skill acquisition. Even 10 focused repetitions per session compounds over time — consistency matters more than volume.

    What positions connect to Granby Roll Advanced?

    BJJ is a linked system. Granby Roll Advanced flows naturally to and from related positions. Study transitions in both directions to build a complete positional game.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: Why does my neck and shoulder get jammed when I try to Granby Roll Advanced: Superior Roll Mechanics from guard?

    This typically happens when your head drives directly into the mat instead of tucking your chin to your shoulder and initiating the roll from your scapula. Ensure your opposite arm is posturing up and creating a fulcrum, allowing your body to pivot around your shoulder joint rather than collapsing your neck.

    Q: How can I generate more momentum for the Granby Roll Advanced: Superior Roll Mechanics when my opponent is much heavier than me?

    Focus on using your hips to drive the initial rotation, creating a powerful 'hip bump' as you initiate the roll. Simultaneously, extend your legs forcefully in the opposite direction of the roll to add linear momentum, leveraging your body's weight against theirs.

    Q: What is the correct angle to aim for when executing the Granby Roll Advanced: Superior Roll Mechanics to avoid getting stuck or swept?

    Aim for a 45-degree angle relative to your opponent's center line, not a direct forward roll. This off-center trajectory allows you to use their weight against them and create space to finish the roll into a dominant position, rather than rolling straight into their defense.

    More Questions

    When is the best time to use the advanced Granby roll to escape bad positions?

    The advanced Granby roll is most effective when you have a slight angle or space to exploit, and your opponent is pressing heavily on you. It's ideal for escaping side control, mount, or even back control when you feel a submission is imminent and a standard escape won't suffice.

    What are the key differences between a basic Granby roll and the advanced version?

    The advanced Granby roll emphasizes superior body mechanics, focusing on generating momentum through hip and shoulder dissociation, and utilizing a powerful leg drive. Unlike a basic roll which might be more of a reactive scramble, the advanced version is a controlled, explosive movement designed to create significant distance and transition opportunities.

    How can I train the advanced Granby roll safely and effectively without a partner?

    You can practice the rotational mechanics and hip/shoulder dissociation on the ground without a partner, focusing on the explosive push-off from your legs. Shadow drilling the movement, visualizing your opponent's pressure and your escape path, will also build muscle memory and timing.
