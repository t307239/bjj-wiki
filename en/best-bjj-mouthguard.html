    FAQ

    Do I need a mouthguard for BJJ?

    Yes. Even in no-gi training, accidental knee and elbow strikes to the jaw are common. A mouthguard protects teeth, jaw, and reduces concussion risk.

    What thickness is best for BJJ?

    1.6–2mm is ideal for BJJ — thin enough for easy breathing and communication while providing protection. Wrestling-style thick guards (4mm+) hinder breathing during rolls.

    How often should I replace my BJJ mouthguard?

    Replace every 6–12 months for regular grapplers, or sooner if you notice cracks, deformation, or persistent odors despite cleaning.
