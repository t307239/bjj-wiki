    📐 Sizing & Care Tips

    Sizing

    Order your normal size then pre-shrink with hot wash + dryer once. Gis typically shrink 3–5%.

    Fit

    Sleeve ends 2" above wrist. Pants end 2" above ankle. Test mobility: hip escape, armbar drill.

    Care

    Cold wash + hang dry extends life. Wash immediately after every session to prevent bacteria.

    IBJJF rules

    Patches must not cover more than 35 cm². Check color rules: white, blue, or black only for IBJJF.

    More Questions

    What is the primary purpose of a BJJ gi?

    A BJJ gi serves multiple purposes, including providing grip points for your opponent to control you, offering protection from mat burns and minor impacts, and signifying your rank within the art. It's also a crucial part of the uniform for competition and training.

    Are there specific brands that are better for beginners?

    For beginners, durability and comfort are key. Brands that offer a good balance of these at a reasonable price point are ideal. Look for pre-shrunk options to avoid sizing issues after washing.

    How important is the fit of a BJJ gi?

    Gi fit is very important for both comfort and effectiveness. A gi that is too loose can give your opponent too many grips, while a gi that is too tight can restrict your movement. It should allow for a full range of motion without excessive slack.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: As a brand new white belt, I'm overwhelmed by all the different BJJ gi brands advertised for 2026. Which ones are actually worth checking out for someone just starting out and what makes them good for beginners?

    For beginners in 2026, focus on brands like Fuji, Hayabusa, and Tatami. These brands generally offer durable, comfortable gis with a good balance of weave weight, making them forgiving for new practitioners while still holding up to regular training. Look for single or lightweight pearl weaves for ease of movement and less restriction.

    Q: I'm trying to decide on a BJJ gi for 2026 but I'm worried about spending a lot of money on something I might outgrow or not like. What are some affordable but still good quality BJJ gi brands that won't break the bank for a beginner?

    Consider brands such as Elite Sports, Sanabul, and Rolljunkie for budget-friendly yet functional gi options in 2026. These brands often use ripstop or lighter cotton weaves, providing good value and durability without the premium price tag of some higher-end manufacturers. They offer a solid starting point for your BJJ journey.

    Q: I've heard that the fit of a BJJ gi is really important, especially when looking at the top brands for 2026. What should a white belt look for in terms of fit to ensure their new gi is comfortable and doesn't hinder their movements during BJJ training?

    For optimal fit in 2026, aim for a gi that allows you to comfortably make a fist with your sleeves when your arms are extended, and the pants should reach your ankles without pooling. The gi jacket should be snug but not constricting across your chest and shoulders, enabling full range of motion for grappling movements.
