    Why Gi Brand Matters

    Your gi is more than just fabric — it's an investment in comfort, durability, and performance on the mat. A quality gi can last years with proper care, while a cheap gi may shrink unpredictably, tear at stress points, or feel restrictive during rolling. The best gi for you depends on budget, weave preference, and fit.

    Premium Gi Brands

    Premium brands like Shoyoroll, Isami, and Tatami offer superior quality, innovative designs, and excellent durability. Shoyoroll is known for limited-edition releases and collectible designs. Isami combines traditional Japanese craftsmanship with modern BJJ needs. Tatami offers competitive pricing for high-end features.

    Mid-Range Gi Brands (Best Value)

    Scramble, Fuji, and Kingz dominate the mid-range market. Scramble offers innovative gi designs and competitive pricing. Fuji provides reliable, durable gis that compete with premium brands at lower cost. Kingz focuses on functionality and durability for serious competitors.

    Budget-Friendly Gi Brands

    Sanabul, Venum, and other budget brands let you train affordably. These gis work fine for beginner training but may not have the longevity of premium brands. Great for kids, beginners, or when you need a backup gi.

    Gi Weave Types Explained

    Single weave: Lightweight and breathable, ideal for hot climates. Double weave: Heavy-duty and durable for intense rolling. Gold weave: Sweet spot between weight and durability. Hybrid: Combines different weaves for specific areas of the gi.

    Size and Fit Guide

    Always check the brand's size chart before ordering. Gis typically shrink 2-4% after washing. Consider your body type: slim-fit gis for lean builds, relaxed-fit for muscular builds. Your sleeve should end 2 inches above your wrist, pants should end 2 inches above your ankle.
