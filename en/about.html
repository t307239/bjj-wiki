Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

Q: Why does my neck hurt so much after trying to escape side control in Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu?

Neck pain during Side Control escapes often stems from overextending your cervical spine and tensing your neck muscles to create space. Instead, focus on driving your shoulder into your opponent's chest and using your hips to shrimp away, keeping your neck in a neutral, relaxed position.

Q: How can I effectively use my hips to generate power when attempting a BJJ sweep from the guard against a larger opponent?

To generate power for a sweep against a bigger opponent, focus on a hip thrust motion. As you initiate the sweep, simultaneously drive your hips upwards towards the ceiling while extending your legs to create a lever, using your opponent's weight against them.

Q: When I'm trying to pass the guard in BJJ, why does my opponent always seem to recover guard even though I feel like I'm in a good position?

Guard recovery is often facilitated by an opponent's ability to create space with their hips and legs. To prevent this, maintain tight control by keeping your weight distributed low and forward, using your forearm to pressure their hip or knee line, and minimizing any gaps they can exploit.
