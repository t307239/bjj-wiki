🥋 BJJ Blue Belt Requirements 2026

The blue belt is the first major milestone in BJJ — and often the hardest to understand because there's no standardized curriculum. Here's what most instructors look for.

Contents

    ⏱️ Time Requirements

    Most students earn their blue belt after 1–2 years of consistent training (3+ days per week). Some academies require a minimum of 6 months at white belt.

    ✅ Technical Requirements

    • Comfortable from all basic guard positions (closed, half, butterfly)
    • Can apply basic submissions: armbar, triangle, rear naked choke, kimura
    • Can escape from mount, side control, and back control
    • Has at least 1–2 reliable guard passes
    • Shows positional awareness — knows where they are and what the threat is
    • Can survive rolling with other blue belts
    • Understands basic takedowns or takedown defense

    🧠 Mindset & Character

    • Shows consistency and dedication to training
    • Taps early and protects training partners
    • Teaches beginners when asked, showing leadership
    • Not ego-driven — can lose without getting upset

    📅 Promotion Timeline

    0–6 months: Learning survival, basic positions, first submissions
    6–12 months: Developing a basic game, improving escapes
    12–24 months: Blue belt territory — consistent game emerging

    🎯 Core Techniques to Master Before Blue Belt

    Armbar Triangle Choke Rear Naked Choke Kimura Hip Escape Bridge & Roll

    ❓ FAQ

    Can I ask my instructor when I'll get my blue belt?

    It's generally frowned upon. Focus on improving, not on the belt. Most instructors promote when they feel you're ready — trust the process.

    Does competition affect blue belt promotion?

    Competition shows character under pressure. While not required at most schools, competing demonstrates your skills in a real setting.

    What's the "blue belt blues"?

    Many blue belts experience frustration around 6–12 months post-promotion. This is normal — you're now aware of how much you don't know. See our Blue Belt Guide.
