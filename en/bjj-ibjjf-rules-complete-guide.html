    More Questions

    What are the most common penalties in IBJJF competition?

    The most common penalties include stalling, illegal moves, and not progressing the match. Understanding these is crucial for avoiding point deductions or even disqualification.

    How do I avoid getting points deducted for inactivity?

    You need to actively try to advance your position, attack submissions, or improve your guard. Simply holding a position without any offensive intent will lead to penalties.

    What are the basic scoring criteria in IBJJF?

    Points are awarded for dominant positions like mount, back control, side control, and knee-on-belly, as well as for sweeps and takedowns. The goal is to achieve and maintain these positions for a minimum of three seconds.
