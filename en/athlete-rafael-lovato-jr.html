    Biography

    Rafael Lovato Jr. is one of the most inspirational figures in martial arts. The first American to win a major international BJJ championship, he went on to become the first American Bellator Middleweight Champion. In 2019, he suffered a life-threatening stroke during training but made a remarkable recovery and returned to coaching. His story of perseverance has inspired millions.

    "BJJ saved my life. Now I use it to help save others."
    — Rafael Lovato Jr.
