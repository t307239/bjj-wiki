Cartwheel Pass: Advanced Technique

Contents

    Overview

    The cartwheel pass is an advanced technique using explosive hip movement and leg positioning to bypass guard defenses.

    Setup Phase

    • Establish control of their legs
    • Position yourself perpendicular to their body
    • Base yourself with both arms

    Explosive Hip Movement

    Drive your hips explosively, rotating your body like a cartwheel. This momentum carries you past their legs and into side control.

    Timing and Positioning

    Attack cartwheel when they are posturing up or when their leg control is loose. The explosiveness overcomes their frame.

    Landing and Control

    Complete the cartwheel into a solid side control or knee on belly position. Recovery from the cartwheel momentum is important for stability.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How long does it take to learn Cartwheel Pass Technique?

    Most practitioners develop functional competency with Cartwheel Pass Technique within 3–6 months of consistent drilling. Mastery — the ability to execute reliably in live rolling against resisting opponents — typically takes 1–2 years.

    Is Cartwheel Pass Technique effective for beginners?

    Yes. Cartwheel Pass Technique is part of the core BJJ curriculum and taught at all belt levels. Beginners should focus on the fundamental mechanics and concepts before refining advanced entries.

    How often should I drill Cartwheel Pass Technique?

    3–5 times per week is ideal for rapid skill acquisition. Even 10 focused repetitions per session compounds over time — consistency matters more than volume.

    What positions connect to Cartwheel Pass Technique?

    BJJ is a linked system. Cartwheel Pass Technique flows naturally to and from related positions. Study transitions in both directions to build a complete positional game.
