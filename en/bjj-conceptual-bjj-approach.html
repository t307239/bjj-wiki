Conceptual BJJ Approach

The conceptual approach to BJJ emphasizes understanding underlying principles rather than memorizing individual techniques. This approach accelerates learning and improves adaptability in live training.

What is Conceptual BJJ

Conceptual BJJ focuses on the 'why' behind techniques rather than the 'how'. By understanding the principles—leverage, base, connection, angles—you can adapt to any situation.

The Principle of Base

Maintaining base means keeping your center of gravity stable. Any technique that disrupts an opponent's base creates opportunities for sweeps and takedowns.

The Principle of Connection

Staying connected to your opponent allows you to feel and react to their movements. Connection applies to both attacking and defending positions.

The Principle of Angles

Most techniques require specific angles to be effective. Learning to create and exploit angles is more valuable than knowing many individual moves.

Applying Concepts to New Situations

When you encounter an unfamiliar position, apply the fundamental concepts. Ask: What is the base? What are the connection points? What angle creates advantage?

Quick Tips

  • Study the principles behind techniques, not just the steps
  • Ask 'why does this work' after learning each technique
  • Look for common principles across different techniques
  • Concepts transfer across gi, no-gi, and MMA
  • Higher belts teach concepts; white belts learn techniques
