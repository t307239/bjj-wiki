🥗 BJJ Diet & Nutrition Guide 2026

Food is fuel. For BJJ athletes training 3–5 days a week, nutrition determines your energy levels, recovery speed, and body composition. Here's what works.

🍗 Macronutrient Blueprint for BJJ

MacroTargetBest Sources
Protein1.6–2.2g per kg bodyweightChicken, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese
Carbohydrates3–5g per kg on training daysRice, oats, sweet potato, fruit
Fat0.8–1.2g per kgAvocado, olive oil, nuts, fatty fish

⏱️ Meal Timing Around Training

3–4 hrs before

Full meal: protein + complex carbs + vegetables

1 hr before

Light snack: banana, rice cake, small smoothie

30–60 min after

Recovery meal: 30g protein + carbs within the anabolic window

Before bed

Casein protein or Greek yogurt for overnight muscle synthesis

⚖️ Weight Cutting for Competition

Warning: Extreme water cutting is dangerous and illegal in some organizations. Focus on walking in at or near competition weight.

Best practice: compete within 2–3kg of your natural walking weight. If cutting is necessary, use water reduction only (no diuretics) and rehydrate fully before competing.
