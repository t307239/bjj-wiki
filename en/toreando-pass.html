Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

Q: Why do I feel a sharp pain in my shoulder when someone tries to armbar me from guard in BJJ?

This pain often occurs when your opponent extends your arm beyond its natural range of motion. To prevent this, keep your elbow bent and tucked close to your body, creating a strong 'L' shape with your arm, and use your free hand to grip their hip or gi to prevent them from creating the necessary leverage for the extension.

Q: How can I effectively escape side control when my training partner is much larger and heavier than me in BJJ?

Focus on creating space by driving your hips into their chest, making it difficult for them to maintain pressure. Simultaneously, use your frames (forearms) to push their head and shoulder away, creating a small gap to shrimp your hips underneath them and begin to recover guard.

Q: What is the correct body positioning to avoid getting swept when someone is attacking my legs from my guard in BJJ?

Maintain a strong base by keeping your hips low and your knees tucked towards your chest, forming a tight guard. When they attempt a sweep, immediately drive your hips into their chest and use your legs to block their hips and prevent them from establishing a dominant angle to unbalance you.
