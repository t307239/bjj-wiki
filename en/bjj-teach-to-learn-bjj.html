    Common Mistakes in Teach To Learn Bjj

    Rushing the Setup

    Attempting to finish before proper mechanics are in place results in failed attempts and positional loss. Prioritize position before submission.

    Using Strength Over Technique

    Muscling through setups creates bad habits and fails against stronger or more skilled opponents. Focus on leverage and angles.

    Skipping Drilling

    Techniques only become available in live rolling after extensive drilling. Regular repetition builds the muscle memory needed for execution under pressure.

    Ignoring Defensive Reactions

    Every technique has common counters. Learn the most frequent defensive reactions and have follow-up attacks ready.

    Common BJJ Problems & FAQ

    Q: Why does my neck hurt when I'm trying to learn the basic BJJ guard retention techniques like shrimping and bridging?

    Neck pain during guard retention often stems from not using your hips and core effectively. Instead of relying on neck tension to create space, focus on driving your hips outward with your glutes and lower back to push your opponent away, while keeping your head and neck relatively neutral.

    Q: How can I effectively learn to defend against a much larger opponent when they are trying to pass my guard in BJJ?

    Against a larger opponent, prioritize using your legs and hips to control their base and structure, rather than trying to overpower them. Focus on maintaining a tight, active guard by keeping your knees tucked and your feet connected to their hips, using your core to generate leverage for sweeps and submissions.

    Q: What is the correct way to use my body to create frames and prevent my opponent from advancing their position when I'm on my back in BJJ?

    To create effective frames, utilize your forearms and shins to create strong, rigid barriers against your opponent's body. Actively push and extend your limbs to maintain distance, ensuring your elbows and knees are bent at angles that distribute force efficiently, preventing them from collapsing your structure.
